pagesnap

Full-page screenshots of JavaScript-rendered sites. Waits for hydration, forces lazy images, freezes animation, then captures.

Try:

Captured screenshots

Give it a FlowHunt flow and a CSV of companies (one per line — name and/or website, extra columns are fine). For each company the flow finds the homepage, about-us and services pages; each is captured full-page as JPEG into a folder named after the company, and the ZIP contains those folders plus a results.xlsx index.

Every product page is captured by default. Each one costs roughly 20–30s and 1.5–2.5 MB, so a company with a long catalogue can take several minutes on its own — set a number here to limit it.
